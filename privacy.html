Fennel – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

Fennel ("the app") is a PCOS diet and symptom tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles and what leaves your device. The short version: the health information you log stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no advertising.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Murat Çay (an individual developer). Contact: dev.murat.cay@gmail.com.

Information you enter (stays on your device)

The app lets you record foods and meals, symptoms, menstrual cycle dates, weight and waist measurements, and your profile choices (PCOS status, goals, eating preferences). This information is stored only on your iPhone. We do not have access to it, we do not upload it to any server, and it is not synced to iCloud. Your PCOS status and logs are health information; we only use them on your device to show your scores, summaries and insights.

If you choose to export a report (for example a PDF to share with your clinician), the file is created on your device and goes only where you send it.

Information that leaves your device

  • Barcode lookups. When you scan a product barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (a public food database) to fetch the product's nutrition facts. No other information, and no information about you, is sent. Foods you search for by name are looked up in a database bundled inside the app, without any network request.
  • Subscriptions. Purchases are handled by Apple, and subscription status is managed with RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier, your purchase and subscription details, and basic device and app information (such as app version, device model and OS version, country/storefront) to confirm whether you have Premium. It does not receive your food, symptom, cycle or weight data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

The camera is used only to scan barcodes on your device. Camera images are not stored or sent anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name or email through the app.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your information for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything the app has stored by going to More → Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone. Because we hold none of your logs, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. Subscription records are held by Apple and RevenueCat and are managed through your Apple ID.

Managing your subscription

You can cancel or change a subscription at any time in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Your rights

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or another region with data protection rights, you can ask about, access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. Since the app keeps your health logs only on your device, you already control them directly. For anything else, contact dev.murat.cay@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

Fennel provides general information and tracking tools. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Talk to a qualified health professional about your health.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for material changes we will also tell you in the app.
