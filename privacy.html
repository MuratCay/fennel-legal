Fennel – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 (version 2.0)

Fennel: PCOS Diet & Tracker ("the app") is a PCOS diet and symptom tracker. This policy explains what the app handles and what leaves your device. The short version: everything you log stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no developer servers, no cloud sync, no analytics and no ads.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Murat Çay (an individual developer). Contact: dev.murat.cay@gmail.com.

Information you enter (stays on your device)

The app stores the following only on your iPhone: meal logs and scores, favourites, symptom log, cycle and period dates, weight and waist measurements, water, supplement entries (name and a "taken" mark only, no doses), weekly meal plan and shopping list ticks, lesson progress, recipe favourites, profile choices (PCOS status, goals, eating preferences, consent date), pregnancy mode (the estimated due date, if you turn it on) and glucose readings.

We have no access to this information. There is no account, no developer server and no iCloud/CloudKit sync. If you use your own device backup (Apple's iCloud or computer backup), this data may be part of that backup. The app does not manage that backup.

The home screen widget shows a summary for today (number of meals, water). It is kept in an App Group storage area on your device and never leaves it.

Your consent

Some of this is health information. The first time you use the app, you are asked to explicitly consent to your health information being stored and processed on your device. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting all data (see below).

Apple Health (optional, off by default)

If you turn it on under More and grant the iOS permission, Fennel can:

  • read your step count, weight and menstrual flow from Apple Health;
  • write only the weight and period days you enter in Fennel.

Apple Health data is not sent to any server, analytics service or third party, and is not used for advertising or marketing. You can withdraw the permission at any time in iOS Settings or the Health app.

Information that leaves your device

Only in these cases:

  • Barcode lookups (Open Food Facts). When you scan a product barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to fetch product information. The camera image never leaves your device. See Open Food Facts' privacy policy.
  • Subscriptions (RevenueCat). Payment is handled by Apple through the App Store; Fennel never sees your card details. To check your subscription status we use RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription information (product, date, status, App Store receipt). It does not receive any health data or anything you log in the app. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Sharing (export). A doctor report PDF or shopping list text leaves your device only if you share it yourself through the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information or share it with anyone, except the two services above (Open Food Facts and RevenueCat) and only for the purposes described.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you.
  • We do not require an account or collect your name or email through the app.

If we ever add privacy-friendly analytics, we will update this policy beforehand, and it will not include personal or health data.

Deleting your data

Go to More → Delete all data to remove all records, the widget summary and settings from your device. Deleting the app also deletes its data. Records Fennel wrote to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request deletion of from the developer.

Managing your subscription

You can cancel or change a subscription at any time in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Your rights

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or another region with data protection rights, you can ask about, access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. Since the app keeps your health logs only on your device, you already control them directly. For anything else, contact dev.murat.cay@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Languages

The app is available in English and German.

Not medical advice

Fennel is a wellness and lifestyle app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Pregnancy mode and the glucose log do not give target values or recommendations. Talk to a qualified health professional about your health.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and for material changes we will also tell you in the app.
